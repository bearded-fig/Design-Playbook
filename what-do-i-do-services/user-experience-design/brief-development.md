# Brief Development

## Purpose

The purpose of brief development is to:

* To make a clear definition between the Needs Assessment which is assumption laden and filled with secondary research and to take the project forward with primary research and engagement
* Develop a realistic/achievable brief after the needs assessment is conducted - this is linked to [Problem Definition.](#user-content-fn-1)[^1]
* Collect all research conducted by the various team members and assimilate it in a logical narrative with a basic plan forward
* Define the users we want to approach: (if we have users at hand then go straight to them, if not develop personas with the goal to find real users)

{% hint style="info" %}
Note: The terms **Needs Assessment** & **Brief Development** are internal jargon used to define and separate project stages that we consider important.
{% endhint %}

## Methodology

It's important to note that the initial needs assessment is almost always with the group who are commissioning the work and is likely built off assumptions that need validation.&#x20;

The salient points from the initial[ **needs assessments** ](#user-content-fn-2)[^2]are broken into their major themes such as:

* Users and their needs
* Pain points (what are we trying to solve)
* Aspects that work (if an existing system or tool)
* Precedent (other examples that may be useful examples to display an idea)

These thematic items are researched, confirmed or denied, then compiled and prioritised into a refined brief or [**SoW**](#user-content-fn-3)[^3] generally created by a project manager.

## What We Need To Do This

* A needs assessment with the partner or client.
* Time to research so we can:
  * Create user personas to guide us to the correct user types
  * Engage with users to verify aspects discussed in the needs assessment.
  * Understand pain points from a user perspective
  * Research the precedent and in some cases run a [**heuristic evaluation**](#user-content-fn-4)[^4] of competitor products
