# Develop User Personas

## Purpose

They are profiles of fictional users whose goals and characteristics represent the needs of a larger group of users. These are useful in the early assumption phase of the project and help us start identifying who we think our user base is.

The purpose of personas are:

* Build empathy for our user groups at and early stage
* To trigger the research phase related to users
* To guide us towards the project appropriate user groups where we can validate our thinking
* Tell stories related to goals or challenges

{% hint style="danger" %}
Personas are a temporary tool to attach to our users early and should be confirmed or denied by real humans and superseded by updated realistic profiles as soon as possible.
{% endhint %}

## Methodology

* In the early stages of a project/product cycle the creation team will name and identify their critical range of user types.&#x20;
* We will annotate what we think personifies these groups. In that we will aim to show things like:
  * Demographic makeup
    * Location
    * Age
    * Education level
    * Occupation
    * Mental and/or physical abilities
    * Gender
    * Race
    * Household or family composition
  * Goals related to this direct project/product
  * Frustrations related to this direct project/product
  * A simple user journey of how the achieve the task at hand
  * Other additional key personal identifiers

{% hint style="info" %}
NOTE: the specific metrics and points used here should be contextual to each project/product
{% endhint %}

## What We Need To Do This

* A completed needs assessment with the partner/client
* We should have carried out contextual research related to our users beforehand
